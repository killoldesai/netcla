# Netofficials homepage concept

## Latest direction: service catalogue first

The current design foregrounds six service groups and 24 service areas immediately after a compact hero. All groups are visible by default, with Software & Websites and Search & Marketing filters. A native disclosure-based services menu exposes the same catalogue. Service selection sets the enquiry category and contextual project prompt; the menu closes on selection, outside click or Escape.

The latest reference review used [ScienceSoft](https://www.scnsoft.com/) for grouped service discovery and [Hidden Brains](https://www.hiddenbrains.com/) for capability prominence. The attached screen informed the dark/ivory editorial rhythm. Brand indigo and lime remain in actions and accents. The illustrative workspace is original concept artwork, not a client project.

The 24 entries expand the five confirmed offerings into proposed scopes: custom software, website design/development, SEO, SEM and PPC. Before publishing, validate every subservice with the delivery team, particularly local SEO, API integrations, workflow automation, remarketing and conversion work. Do not treat this design expansion as proof of specialist delivery or create a separate SEO landing page for every entry. Link to existing relevant service pages where approved; prototype links currently route to the form. AI, cloud, cybersecurity, mobile and industry expertise are not newly claimed by this prototype.

Checks cover the 24 visible service links, group filters, mega menu, enquiry routing, mobile navigation and preview submission. Responsive overflow was checked at 320, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 pixels. The form remains a local demonstration and sends no enquiry.

Open `index.html` to review the responsive layout. The revised direction uses an immersive navy hero, oversized typography, original indigo/lime ribbon artwork, lime actions and alternating editorial sections. All styling and interaction are local; no external fonts or scripts are required.

Visual references reviewed: [Hidden Brains](https://www.hiddenbrains.com/) for its dark opening and prominent capability/CTA hierarchy; [Infosys](https://www.infosys.com/) for immersive presentation and large typography. The composition, artwork and copy are original to this concept. No reference-site imagery, client evidence or business claims were reused.

The page sequence is navigation, build-and-growth hero, core capabilities, two service branches, an interactive business-need selector, connected delivery proposition, project approach, FAQs, enquiry form and footer. Service links and selector choices set the corresponding enquiry type. The hero's original ribbon illustration connects the two service branches visually.

This is a design prototype. The form validates inputs locally and shows an explicit preview message; it does not send or store data. Before launch, connect an approved enquiry endpoint, privacy notice, CRM routing, spam controls and successful-submission tracking. Confirm the actual delivery process and international working arrangements described in the copy.

Keep existing service URLs when integrating this layout. Prototype service links currently route to the form; production links should lead to the agreed service pages. Add About and verified portfolio navigation when those destinations are ready. Insert a selected-work section after services when real publishable projects are approved, with project scope and links to case studies. Client logos, numerical results, certifications and testimonials are intentionally absent pending evidence.

Checked in headless Edge at desktop width 1440px and mobile width 390px: horizontal overflow, service selection, menu state and demo-form behavior. These checks do not constitute a full accessibility or production integration audit.
