Before we talk

Your project.
Your questions.

A few practical answers before you share your brief.

How much does custom software development cost?

The estimate depends on scope, complexity, integrations, user roles and delivery requirements. Share your priorities and any budget range so we can discuss a practical starting scope.

Can I start without a complete specification?

Yes. A description of the business problem, intended users and current process is enough to begin a scoping conversation.

Can you work with an existing system?

Share what you have, how it is used and what needs to improve. Access, architecture, dependencies and feasibility need review before a scope is agreed.

How long will the project take?

Timing depends on the agreed features, dependencies, review cycles and available delivery capacity. Milestones should be established after the requirements are understood.

Who owns the source code and deliverables?

Ownership, source-code access, third-party licences and handover terms should be agreed in the project contract before work starts.

What happens after launch?

Discuss handover, maintenance, support and future improvements during scoping. Ongoing work and responsibilities should be agreed explicitly.
