Before we talk

Your project.
Your questions.

A few practical answers before you share your brief.

How much do SEO services cost?

Fees depend on the agreed audit, technical work, content, reporting and implementation responsibilities. Share your website and goals so we can discuss an appropriate engagement.

Can I engage you just for SEO?

Yes. SEO can be discussed as a standalone engagement for your existing website. Development work can be scoped separately if the audit identifies a need.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

We welcome international enquiries. Target countries, audiences and search priorities should be agreed for your specific engagement.

How long does SEO take?

Timing varies with your starting point, competition and implementation. Set review milestones and assess relevant visibility and enquiries; rankings, traffic and lead volumes cannot be guaranteed.

What access will you need?

Access requirements depend on the scope. Search Console, analytics and appropriate website access can help establish a baseline. Agree permissions and responsibilities before work starts.

How will we measure success?

Agree what counts as a qualified organic enquiry, then review visibility, relevant visits and conversion data. Test submissions and unrelated enquiries should be distinguished from business leads.
