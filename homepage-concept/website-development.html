Before we talk

Your project.
Your questions.

A few practical answers before you share your brief.

How much does website development cost?

The estimate depends on page templates, content, design requirements, functionality and integrations. Share your priorities and any budget range so we can discuss a practical starting scope.

Can I start without a complete specification?

Yes. A description of the business problem, intended users and current process is enough to begin a scoping conversation.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Share your current URL, the platform it uses and what you want to improve. Existing pages, functionality and URL changes should be reviewed before a redesign scope is agreed.

How long will the project take?

Timing depends on the agreed features, dependencies, review cycles and available delivery capacity. Milestones should be established after the requirements are understood.

Who provides the website content?

Content creation, editing, imagery and approvals should be agreed during scoping. Share any existing copy and brand assets so responsibilities are clear.

Does website development include SEO?

Agree the technical and on-page requirements included in the website scope. Ongoing SEO and paid search can be discussed as separate engagements; rankings and lead volumes are not guaranteed.
