Before we talk

Your project.
Your questions.

A few practical answers before you share your brief.

How much does PPC management cost?

Management fees depend on campaign scope, account complexity and agreed responsibilities. Advertising spend is separate, and landing-page or tracking work should be specified in the scope.

Can you manage an existing advertising account?

Share the account platform, target markets and what needs to improve. An account review helps establish a practical scope and access requirements.

Can you run campaigns for international buyers?

We welcome international enquiries. Target locations, campaign language, time zones, budgets and account requirements should be agreed for your engagement.

Do you guarantee leads or return on ad spend?

No. Results depend on demand, competition, your offer, budget and the enquiry journey. Agree meaningful conversion events and review lead quality alongside campaign performance.

What access will you need?

Depending on the scope, advertising account, analytics, tag management and website access may be needed. Agree permissions and responsibilities before work starts.

How will we measure success?

Agree what counts as a qualified paid-search enquiry. Review spend, tracked conversions, cost per qualified enquiry and lead quality; distinguish test submissions and irrelevant leads.
